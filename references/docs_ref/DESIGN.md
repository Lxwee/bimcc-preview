# BIMCC Web Visual System

<!-- impeccable:design-system 1 -->

## Direction

The approved direction is a dark fluid-glass enterprise technology website for BIMCC.

It combines:

- Deep blue aurora background.
- Crisp glass panels with one-pixel translucent borders.
- Large strategic spacing with dense but readable module interiors.
- Platform cockpit visuals as the primary trust signal.
- Engineering project evidence, approved partner logos, product screenshots, and real contact paths.
- A restrained deep-space product focus inspired by Raycast, Reflect, AuthKit, and Scale with Teddy.

## Mode

Persuade.

Visitors should quickly understand that BIMCC is an engineering digitalization platform and service company with real products, project delivery experience, and credible enterprise contact channels.

## Palette

- Page base: `#0b1121`.
- Deep surface: `#111a2e`.
- Glass surface: white at 5-10% opacity.
- Primary accent: cyan blue `#38bdf8`.
- Secondary accent: indigo blue `#6366f1`.
- Success accent: emerald `#34d399`, used only for verified or evidence-oriented highlights.
- Text primary: near white.
- Text secondary: slate blue gray.
- Local signal colors may appear only inside relationship nodes or subtle material light, never as competing CTA colors.

## Typography

- Chinese-first system sans: PingFang SC, HarmonyOS Sans SC, Microsoft YaHei, system UI.
- No `font-light`.
- Body copy uses 15-16px with generous line height.
- Display copy is bold, crisp, and controlled. Avoid oversized all-caps treatment.

## Components

- Navigation is fixed or sticky glass with blur, one-pixel border, and compact height.
- CTA buttons use a cyan-to-indigo gradient, clear contrast, and single-line labels.
- Cards use `glass-card`: translucent surface, backdrop blur, inset highlight, one-pixel border, and subtle hover lift.
- Data proof blocks use large tabular numerals and short evidence labels.
- Case cards should use official website and brochure material where available.
- Partner logos from the current official homepage partner section are approved for this prototype direction.
- Contact modules use real phone and address details from the current official contact page.
- Contact QR positions should reuse the current official contact page assets.
- Lead forms in the static prototype reserve an integration seam only; real submission is handled by the production CMS or backend.
- Hero motion uses one product-centered focal sequence: a quiet background field, a BIM / GIS / IoT / AI / Twin relationship orbit, and restrained button shimmer.
- Secondary pages inherit the same deep-space product system through lightweight signal maps in the page hero.

## Reference Direction

- Raycast contributes the memorable first-viewport focal object.
- Reflect contributes quiet connected-material motion and layered product depth.
- AuthKit contributes subtle button shimmer, calm background movement, and precise component states.
- Scale with Teddy contributes controlled color richness, used as small signal accents only.

## Motion Thesis

The homepage should feel alive because the product system is connected, not because every section is animated. The strongest motion belongs in the hero. Supporting motion is limited to material breathing, scroll reveal, hover feedback, and form status response.

Secondary pages use slower signal drift only. Their job is continuity and product trust, not a second hero spectacle.

## Constraints

- Keep the homepage IA at 8 modules.
- Keep top navigation labels stable: 首页, 产品平台, 解决方案, 客户案例, 技术与研发, 资源中心, 关于筑云, 联系我们.
- Do not use the 2014 founding date in Hero metrics.
- Do not invent new metrics, customer names, awards, or contact channels.
- Homepage partner logos are approved by the user for this redesign direction.
- Case outcomes, product screenshots, and project imagery may reference the official website and brochure.
- Keep source traceability for claims and images during production implementation.
- The redesigned website may replace all previous public website content.
- Preserve or redirect important legacy URLs during production migration.
- This is a local static prototype. Formal implementation may later migrate to the production website stack or CMS.
